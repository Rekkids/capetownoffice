Who they're for

Pick the one that matches your day

Need to focus at a desk → The 9/5
Budget-conscious, strictly office hours, don't need evenings or weekends. The 9/5 gets you in the door at our lowest rate.
Just need a base between meetings → Common Room
Back-to-back meetings across the city, or mostly working elsewhere. R895 buys a proper base camp instead of a coffee shop tab.
Want community without a desk → Common Room
You don't need a workstation, but you want the events, the WhatsApp group, and a room full of people worth knowing.

You've been buying day passes here and there. Whichever you use more -- the desk or the kitchen -- works out cheaper as a monthly rate.
