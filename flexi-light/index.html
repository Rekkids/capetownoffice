How it works

Same Flexi model. A tighter cycle.

Flexi Light works like our Hybrid membership: pick an hour allowance and the clock only runs while you're connected. The difference is the cycle it runs on and what's included.

Your 30-day cycle starts the day you sign up and runs to the same date the following month -- not a calendar month. Unused hours don't carry over and there's no part-month or pro-rata payment: each cycle is paid in full, and public holidays aren't counted as access days.

Flexi Light also seats you in our main open workspace rather than the premium desk areas, and meeting rooms and the podcast studio are booked and paid for as you use them -- not included for free. There's no access fob, so it's daytime access only, not 24/7.

What you get in return: the lowest hourly rate we offer at CTO. If an unpredictable trip, a weekend recording session or holiday access matters to you, Hybrid's 60-day window is built for exactly that -- see how it compares.

Who it's for

Budget-conscious, still wants a real desk

The student or early-career professional
You need a real desk to focus, but a full Hybrid or dedicated membership is more than your budget needs right now. Flexi Light gets you in the door at our lowest rate.
The occasional meeting-room user
You mostly just need to sit and work -- meeting rooms and the podcast studio are a nice-to-have, not a daily need. Paying per use costs you less than paying for it every month.
The daytime-only worker
You're never here at night or on weekends anyway. No access fob means no extra cost for access hours you'd never use.
The day-pass regular, ready to commit
You've been buying day passes here and there. An hour block works out cheaper the moment you're in more than once a week.
