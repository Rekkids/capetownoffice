What this means for you

The short version

Looking for a space from December?

We won't be here, so it's best to look elsewhere. We'd rather tell you now than have you find out after you've settled in.

Need a desk before then?

You're very welcome. Desks, meeting rooms, the podcast studio, the fibre and the coffee all run as normal until 27 November. Month to month, so there's nothing to get out of. And if you'd rather not start somewhere that's closing, we completely understand.
